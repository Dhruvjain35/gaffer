## Design Context (v3 — claude.ai warmth × FIFA 2026, 2026-06-26)

Pivot AGAIN: drop the cool editorial serif look. Target = the **claude.ai / Anthropic feel** — warm, huge, *inviting*, friendly yet premium — but the content is GAFFER and the theme is FIFA World Cup 2026. It should feel like a site FIFA would buy: confident scale, warmth, soccer energy, smooth premium motion. Zero AI-slop.

### Users
Hackathon judges (design-award caliber expected) + World Cup fans. First impression must feel *huge and inviting*, like Claude's homepage — a big warm welcome, not a dashboard.

### Brand Personality
**Warm · confident · alive.** The friendliness and openness of claude.ai, the bold energy of a World Cup. Big, generous, welcoming. The cleverness (it checks/coaches itself) is present but the feeling is "come on in."

### Aesthetic Direction
Claude's warm palette: **cream/ivory** base, **clay/coral** primary accent (the Claude orange), warm dark-brown ink. A **pitch green** as a soccer-grounding secondary (verified / on the pitch). Soft rounded shapes, big type, a **big inviting question input as the hero centerpiece** (claude.ai signature). Subtle warm ambient motion (slow drifting soft gradient warmth — never gaudy). Host-nation energy (🇺🇸🇨🇦🇲🇽, WORLD CUP 26). Keep the multi-screen app (Home · Ask · How it works · Venues). Anti-reference: cool editorial serif, dark+neon, dense telemetry, anything that reads "AI generated."

### Typography (warm, friendly, non-reflex)
- **Display / headlines:** Schibsted Grotesk (600–800) — warm, confident, modern; huge sizes.
- **Body / UI:** Hanken Grotesk (400–600) — clean warm humanist sans.

### Design Principles
1. **Huge & inviting first.** The home is a big warm welcome with a prominent ask-anything input. Scale = confidence.
2. **Warm, not cold.** Cream + clay + warm ink. The accent is clay/coral; green only for "verified/pitch" moments.
3. **Soft & rounded.** Generous radii, friendly buttons, big inputs — the claude.ai softness.
4. **Calm premium motion.** One warm ambient drift + smooth staggered reveals + buttery hovers. No loops that distract, no shockwaves.
5. **FIFA energy, tastefully.** Host flags, WC26 nods, soccer warmth — present, never gimmicky.
6. **Still multi-screen, still progressive disclosure** for the trust/observability detail.
