## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences, one screen. **Hackathon judges** (Arize/Google engineers, async, June 22–Jul 6, poking the live URL + repo) are the real evaluators. **World Cup 2026 fans** are the nominal users asking matchday questions. The interface must read instantly as a serious, alive product to a skeptical engineer — not a chatbot demo.

### Brand Personality
**Tactical · broadcast · self-correcting.** A football-manager's instinct fused with a mission-control telemetry rig. The hero is not "World Cup trivia" — it's **an AI agent you can watch referee itself and get smarter in real time.** Every answer is scored for honesty on-screen; misses get coached out, with evidence. The product should feel like a live broadcast control room, not an app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, by context (an observability/broadcast surface read by engineers). Broadcast-graphics + mission-control: lower-thirds, a live form guide, a scorebar, telemetry rows, drawn pitch geometry. Evolve the existing pitch-green + acid-lime identity in OKLCH. **Anti-reference: a generic AI chat UI** (centered bubbles, lavender gradients, glassmorphism, rounded-rect cards with drop shadows, cyan-on-dark). It must pass the "how was this made?" test, never "which AI made this?".

### Typography (deliberate, non-reflex — IBM Plex Mono is banned and removed)
- **Display / scoreboard:** Saira Condensed (700–900) — athletic, technical, broadcast numerals.
- **Body / UI:** Hanken Grotesk (400–700) — refined humanist grotesque, high readability.
- **Telemetry / data:** Spline Sans Mono (400–600) — contemporary technical mono for traces, versions, tool calls.

### Design Principles
1. **The honesty score is the hero.** Every answer wears a live GOAL/MISS broadcast verdict; the running form guide is always visible. Trust is the product.
2. **Telemetry, not chat.** Tool calls, Phoenix traces, playbook versions are first-class broadcast graphics, not hidden logs.
3. **One orchestrated load, restrained micro-motion.** Pitch draws in, scorebar settles, panels stagger (ease-out-expo). Verdicts stamp. No bounce, no scattered fidgets.
4. **Asymmetry + rhythm.** Break the grid for the scorebar and lower-thirds; vary spacing for hierarchy. No uniform card grids, no centered everything.
5. **Acid lime is rare.** 60/30/10 — dark green surfaces, dim-green structure, acid only for the live/now signal.
