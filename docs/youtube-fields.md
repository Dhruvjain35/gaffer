**Title:**
GAFFER — the World Cup concierge that coaches itself | Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon

**Description:**
A World Cup 2026 fan concierge that gets measurably better while you watch.

The Player (Google ADK + Gemini on Vertex AI) answers fan questions grounded in a verified
knowledge base. A Gemini judge scores every answer live and files the verdict to Arize
Phoenix on the exact trace. Then the Gaffer, a second agent working Arize's Phoenix MCP
server, reviews the failed traces, drills every miss into a regression dataset, rewrites
the playbook, tests old vs new in judge-scored Phoenix experiments, and promotes the winner
to production. No human, no redeploy.
